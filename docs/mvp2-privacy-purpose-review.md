# MVP2 — Privacy purpose review

Status: draft, not approved for publication or database seeding.

| Feature | Candidate basis | Review required |
|---|---|---|
| Account, agenda, tasks | Contract performance | Necessity and retention |
| Requested AI assistance | Contract performance where necessary | Provider disclosure and minimization |
| Optional AI personalization | Consent where applicable | Separate opt-in and withdrawal |
| Sensitive health data | Specific highlighted consent where applicable | Article 11 review, minimization and retention |
| Optional marketing | Consent | Separate opt-in and withdrawal |
| Security and fraud prevention | Applicable statutory basis | Necessity, balancing and sensitive-data review |
| Support | Contract performance where necessary | Retention and access |

Do not activate purpose keys, seed official notices, or enable production gates from this draft. Before integration, approve purpose identifiers, versions, legal basis, data categories, recipients, retention and withdrawal effects. Cancellation and privacy rights must remain available without optional consent. Authenticated staging requires isolated credentials.
